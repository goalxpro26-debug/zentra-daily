Grand Theft Auto VI is the biggest launch in PlayStation history, Sony said on Monday, with more than 39 million unique players playing the game on PlayStation 5 in its first four days.

Players logged 683 million hours between November 19 and 22 — about 17 hours each — and Sony said the game set a new record for the most players online at the same time on PlayStation. It was also the first game played on 70 percent of new PS5 consoles set up during launch week, and Sony said the release drove the largest single-day surge in newly connected consoles outside the year-end holidays.

The launch dominated streaming as well. According to streaming data from Streams Charts, reported by The Guardian, viewers watched 364.5 million hours of GTA VI content in the two days after release — roughly 41,000 years of footage. The game drew more viewership than the next three titles combined, and more than twice the launch viewership of Valorant, making it the most-watched game release on record.

## The sales behind the players

The player counts follow the official sales figures Take-Two Interactive announced last week: $3.79 billion in day-one retail sales and 32.5 million copies sold before release, the biggest entertainment launch in history across any medium. The figures are the company's own estimates of what players paid in shops and online stores, and they replaced early third-party estimates of about $2.36 billion.

The two sets of numbers measure different things, and should not be added together. Sony counts players, not copies sold: its 39 million covers PlayStation 5 only, over four days, and counts unique accounts rather than purchases. Take-Two's $3.79 billion covers every platform on launch day alone. The company has not split its total by console or between digital and boxed copies.

## Thirteen years in the making

The scale reflects both the wait and the spend behind the game. GTA VI is the first new entry in the series in 13 years, and Take-Two is estimated to have spent $1 billion or more developing it, making it one of the most expensive entertainment projects ever made. For comparison, its predecessor, Grand Theft Auto V, made about $800 million on its first day in 2013. Critics have been nearly unanimous as well: the game holds a 99 on review aggregator Metacritic, tying the highest score ever recorded there.

For now the game is available only on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, with no PC version announced, and Take-Two has said little about the online multiplayer mode analysts expect in 2027. But whether measured in sales, players or viewers, the launch has already set records on every count released so far.
