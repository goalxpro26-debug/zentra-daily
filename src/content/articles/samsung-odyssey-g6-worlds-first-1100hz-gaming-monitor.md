Samsung has put a price and a date on the fastest gaming monitor it has ever made. The 27-inch Odyssey G6, known as the G60H, will reach retailers across the United States in December priced at $899.99, the company announced on Monday.

The headline figure is a refresh rate of up to 1,100Hz — the number of times per second the screen can redraw its image. Samsung describes it as the first gaming monitor in the world to reach that speed. But the four-digit figure comes with a trade-off that buyers will need to understand: the monitor only hits 1,100Hz in HD mode, at a lower resolution. At its native QHD resolution of 2,560 by 1,440 pixels, the refresh rate tops out at 600Hz.

That split is the work of a feature Samsung calls Dual Mode, which lets players switch between the two settings depending on the game. A fast competitive shooter, where spotting movement a fraction of a second sooner matters, suits the 1,100Hz mode. A slower, visually rich open-world game suits the sharper QHD picture at 600Hz — itself far above the 144Hz or 240Hz most gaming monitors offer.

## A new blur-reduction feature

The G6 also introduces Odyssey Clear Motion, a blur-reduction system that Samsung says keeps fast-changing scenes clear during critical moments of play. The company plans to bring the feature to more of its gaming monitors after its debut here.

The panel is a fast IPS display with a 1ms grey-to-grey response time, and it supports HDR10+ Gaming, which adjusts brightness and contrast in real time. It works with Nvidia G-Sync and AMD FreeSync Premium to reduce tearing and stutter, and it connects through one DisplayPort 2.1 port and two HDMI 2.1 ports.

The launch has been a long time coming. Samsung first showed the monitor in December 2025, when the highest refresh rate it quoted was 1,040Hz. By August, at the Gamescom trade show, that figure had risen to 1,100Hz, while the QHD figure stayed at 600Hz.

The monitor arrives into a small but intensifying race. LG's UltraGear 25G590B tops out at 1,000Hz, and Samsung's new model undercuts it on price by about $100. Samsung says it has led the global gaming monitor market for seven consecutive years, from 2019 through 2025, according to IDC figures.

## What buyers should check

Not every setup will reach the headline speeds. Samsung notes that macOS does not support Dual Mode or the 600Hz maximum, and that AMD FreeSync Premium does not operate at 1,100Hz. Older AMD Radeon RX 6-series cards and below cannot reach 1,000Hz or higher.
