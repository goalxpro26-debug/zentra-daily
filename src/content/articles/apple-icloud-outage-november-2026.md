Apple confirmed an outage affecting several of its iCloud services on Tuesday, November 10, leaving some users unable to reach their mail, calendars and photos for part of the day.

The disruption was reported by 9to5Mac on Tuesday afternoon, and Apple's own System Status page listed a string of iCloud services as affected. By the early evening, most of them had been restored — but two problems were still active.

## What went down

According to Apple's System Status page, the outage touched iCloud Mail, iCloud Calendar, iCloud Contacts and Photos, along with Game Center, iWork for iCloud, Apple Music and Podcasts. As of 5:07 p.m. Pacific time on Tuesday, each of those was listed as resolved.

Two services were still showing problems at that time. iCloud Account & Sign In remained in outage, and Find My was listed as having an issue. Other iCloud services — including iCloud Drive, iCloud Backup, iCloud Keychain, iCloud Notes and iCloud Web Apps — were shown as available.

The status page does not say how many people were affected or when the outage began, and Apple has not given a cause for the disruption.

## What is still affected

The continuing outage in iCloud Account & Sign In means some users may still have trouble signing in to iCloud or reaching account-related features. The ongoing issue with Find My may affect locating devices through the service.

For most users, the practical effects of the earlier outage were interruptions to everyday tools: mail not syncing, calendar entries not updating across devices, and photos not uploading. iCloud sits at the centre of how Apple devices share data, so even a partial outage can be felt across an iPhone, a Mac and the web at the same time.

## What remains unknown

Apple has not said what caused the outage, how widespread it was, or when the two remaining problems will be cleared. The company's status page is the main public record of the incident, and it lists only whether each service is available, affected or in outage — not the number of users involved.

Users still seeing problems can check the System Status page for the latest state of each service, and the page is updated as incidents are resolved.
