The Buffalo Bills beat the Kansas City Chiefs 13–10 on Thanksgiving night, with kicker Tyler Bass drilling a 57-yard field goal as time expired at a rain-soaked Highmark Stadium in Orchard Park, New York.

The winning kick came with four seconds on the clock. Kansas City had been forced to punt from its own 11-yard line with 15 seconds remaining, and Matt Araiza's punt was partially tipped by Buffalo's Joe Andreessen. Greg Dortch fielded the line-drive ball and returned it 17 yards to the Kansas City 39, giving Bass his chance from near midfield. On a field torn up by hours of rain, Bass found a patch of ground he trusted, holder Tommy Doman set the ball down, and the kick sailed through.

Bass had already rescued Buffalo once earlier in the fourth quarter, hitting a 54-yard field goal to tie the game at 10–10. Minutes later, Kansas City kicker Harrison Butker missed a 46-yard attempt wide right — his eighth missed field goal of the season — and the Chiefs never got the ball back in scoring range.

It was a defensive slog from the start, played in freezing rain, wind and cold. James Cook ran for 117 yards and scored Buffalo's only touchdown on a five-yard run in the second quarter. Neither star quarterback found a rhythm in the conditions: Josh Allen endured one of the roughest outings of his career, while Patrick Mahomes fumbled on an aborted snap in the closing minutes. The two teams combined for a game decided almost entirely by field position and special teams.

The win lifted Buffalo to 10–1, the best record in the AFC, and extended the Bills' winning streak to seven games. It also kept them in the driver's seat for the conference's No. 1 seed and gave them the head-to-head tiebreaker over Kansas City. The Chiefs fell to 8–3 and now trail the Denver Broncos in the AFC West.

Bass said afterwards that the hardest part of the final kick was not the wind, which had eased, or the rain, but finding a decent spot on the chewed-up turf. "I just kind of saw a spot that somewhat looked pretty good," he said. "Just kind of made sure the footing was okay, and after that, just let it ride." It was enough to close out the Thanksgiving tripleheader with the night's most dramatic finish.
