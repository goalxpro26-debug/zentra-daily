After 13 years, one of entertainment's biggest releases is finally here. "Grand Theft Auto VI" launches Thursday, sending players back to Vice City and testing whether Rockstar Games can follow one of the bestselling video games ever made with another cultural and commercial phenomenon.

The game becomes playable at midnight local time on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, so players in Asia and other regions will begin entering the game hours before its release in the United States. New Zealand is among the first countries where players can get in.

The scale of the launch is hard to overstate. The game file is 212 gigabytes, and one New Zealand network expects about 35 petabytes of additional traffic across its system as downloads begin.

The expectations are enormous, too. The "Grand Theft Auto" franchise has sold more than 470 million units worldwide, while "Grand Theft Auto V," released in 2013, accounts for more than 230 million of those sales and reached $1 billion in retail sales in just three days. Pre-orders for the new game are estimated to have already passed $1.5 billion, according to market intelligence firm Sensor Tower.

Rockstar is returning to Vice City, its fictional version of Miami, with two new protagonists and a sprawling Florida-inspired world. At the centre of "GTA VI" are Lucia Caminos and Jason Duval, a couple whose attempts to improve their lives pull them deeper into the criminal underworld of Leonida, Rockstar's fictional version of Florida. Their relationship marks one of the biggest departures from "Grand Theft Auto V," which followed three playable protagonists. Rockstar has promoted Leonida as its largest and most detailed open world yet, stretching beyond Vice City into beaches, highways, wetlands, nightlife districts and smaller communities inspired by South Florida.

Miami has leaned into its connection to Vice City ahead of the release. Miami-Dade County entered into a $3.9 million promotional deal with Rockstar that includes a giant "Welcome to Vice City" display atop the Miami Heat's Kaseya Center. The campaign has not been universally welcomed — some South Florida officials have questioned whether public resources should be used to promote a franchise known for car theft, gunplay and other criminal activity.

Few entertainment releases arrive with this much anticipation. The first "GTA VI" trailer drew more than 93 million YouTube views in its first 24 hours, and the second generated more than 475 million views across platforms in a day. The game has also received eight nominations for the Game Awards, including game of the year, with the awards presented on December 10. Rockstar also commissioned an original adaptive score from Grammy-winning French electronic duo Justice, with a 30-track soundtrack due December 11.

Despite years of trailers and promotional material, Rockstar has kept significant portions of "GTA VI" under wraps, including the full voice cast. Those answers will begin emerging first from players in Asia and Europe as midnight arrives in their regions, followed by North America hours later.
