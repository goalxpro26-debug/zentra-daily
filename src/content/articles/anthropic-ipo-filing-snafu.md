Anthropic, the company behind the Claude chatbot, sent bankers an IPO prospectus on Monday showing a share sale of about 867 million shares priced at $70 to $80 — then pulled it back hours later, saying the details were inaccurate.

The circulated document implied a fundraising of up to $69.3 billion at the top of the price range. A follow-up email told recipients the pricing details were wrong and that an updated filing would follow, without saying which figures were incorrect. An Anthropic spokesperson declined to comment.

## A clerical error before a giant listing

The retracted document was an updated prospectus sent by Anthropic's outside counsel in Canada, according to The Information, citing people familiar with the matter. The counsel later told the banks that some of the details were inaccurate and that the filing would be updated.

The mix-up landed just as Anthropic was preparing to begin formal marketing of what would be one of the largest public listings ever. Marketing is expected to start on Tuesday morning, according to IFR, with one-on-one and group meetings between management and investors around the world, in person and virtually. Retail investors may receive as much as 20% of the available shares, people familiar with the matter have said.

If the timetable holds, Anthropic would price the offering and debut on the Nasdaq Global Select Market in the week of November 16 — before the US Thanksgiving holiday on November 26, the company's apparent deadline.

## A nervous market for AI stocks

The error added last-minute drama to an already jittery market. AI-related stocks sold off sharply last week, briefly pushing the Cboe Volatility Index — Wall Street's "fear gauge" — above 25, a level at which companies typically postpone listings.

The valuation debate is unresolved. Some potential investors have discussed a range of about $1.1 trillion to $1.3 trillion, well below where talks had been focused a month earlier, during which time the Nasdaq 100 index fell 14%. The retracted $69.3 billion figure would have fallen short of an earlier goal of matching SpaceX's record-setting debut.

Anthropic also faces questions about its growth: its quarterly revenue growth slowed at the end of the third quarter, and the data centres powering its models could face pressure after the Democrats' midterm election gains — the trigger for last week's sell-off.

Still, advisers say the deal's sheer size may insulate it. "It's such a big deal and such a unique asset that if the company wants to move forward, they can," Matt Kennedy, senior strategist at IPO research firm Renaissance Capital, said. "It just becomes a matter of where it prices."

## What is still unknown

It is not clear which details in the retracted filing were wrong — the share count, the price range, or both — and Anthropic has not said when the corrected filing will arrive. The final size and pricing of the offering remain unsettled, and the company has not confirmed the marketing schedule publicly.
