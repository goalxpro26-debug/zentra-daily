Ukraine struck the Kerch Bridge before dawn on Tuesday, collapsing a section of its road span into the sea in what Kyiv described as its most significant attack yet on the link between Russia and occupied Crimea.

President Volodymyr Zelenskyy called the operation "unprecedented", saying Ukrainian forces had spent weeks wearing down Russian air defences around the bridge before bombarding it overnight. Ukraine's General Staff said an arch and a span of the road section had collapsed, severing the road link, while the railway line was still standing, though its arch was damaged.

"The symbol of the occupation has cracked," the General Staff said in a statement.

The 19-kilometre bridge, opened by Vladimir Putin in 2018, is the only bridge connecting Crimea — illegally annexed by Russia in 2014 — to the Russian mainland, and a key supply route to the peninsula. A graphic published by The New York Times, based on imagery from the Ukrainian Security Service, showed a collapsed section of about 700 feet, with debris in the water below.

Zelenskyy said the attack was the seventh and final stage of an operation carried out over several weeks: Ukrainian forces first knocked out air-defence systems, radars and electronic-warfare equipment, then domestically developed long-range drones struck the road section and Neptune missiles targeted the rail section, with Flamingo precision-guided missiles delivering the final blow.

Russia confirmed the attack. Its Investigative Committee said drones had struck the bridge and caused damage, without elaborating. Sergei Aksyonov, the Russian-installed governor of Crimea, said traffic was halted but rail traffic had been restored by Tuesday afternoon. Kremlin spokesperson Dmitry Peskov said the bridge would be restored, "even better than it was before".

It is the latest in a series of Ukrainian attempts to destroy the bridge: a truck bomb in 2022, unmanned naval vessels in 2023, and explosives planted on its undersea supports two years later — none of which put it out of action for long.

## Timing, and a night of strikes in both directions

The attack came two days before Putin is due to meet Donald Trump and Xi Jinping in Shenzhen on Thursday, where the war in Ukraine is expected to be discussed. Trump has said he plans to raise the war with both leaders.

The same night, Russia fired seven ballistic missiles at two of Kyiv's main bridges over the Dnipro, killing at least two people in the capital, according to Zelenskyy. Russia's Defence Ministry said its forces had downed 1,441 Ukrainian drones overnight — the second straight night of record interception claims.
