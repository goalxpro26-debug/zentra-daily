The Chicago Bears stunned the Detroit Lions 39–35 on Thanksgiving Day, scoring the winning touchdown with three seconds left at Ford Field to take sole possession of first place in the NFC North.

Trailing 35–32 with no timeouts remaining, quarterback Caleb Williams floated a six-yard pass to the back-right corner of the end zone, where Rome Odunze hauled it in and kept both feet in bounds. The score stood after a review and gave Chicago its first lead of the entire game.

The Bears had trailed 35–24 midway through the fourth quarter after Jahmyr Gibbs broke free for a 70-yard touchdown run, his third score of the game. Williams answered at once, hitting Odunze for a 41-yard touchdown and then running in the two-point conversion to cut the gap to 35–32 with 7:20 left. Detroit could not run out the clock: a pair of failed fourth-down attempts handed the ball back to Chicago with just over two minutes remaining.

The final drive nearly stalled before it started. Williams converted a fourth-and-1 with a designed bootleg run, Detroit was flagged 15 yards for a facemask on D. J. Reed, and D'Andre Swift picked up key yardage before Williams found Odunze in the corner on third-and-goal. "Rome is not even the primary on that one," head coach Ben Johnson said afterwards. "Caleb did a good job, just working through it, and the guys gave him a clean pocket."

Williams finished 30 of 44 for 345 yards and two touchdowns, with a 106.7 passer rating. Odunze caught six passes for 87 yards and both scores. Swift added 65 rushing yards and a touchdown. For Detroit, Jared Goff threw for 313 yards and a touchdown, and Amon-Ra St. Brown caught seven passes for 146 yards.

The win lifted Chicago to 9–2 and a sixth straight victory, while handing Detroit its first loss since Week 4. It was Williams's first win over the Lions, and only the third time a team has rallied from 11 or more points down in the fourth quarter to win on Thanksgiving — the 1980 Bears did it against Detroit, and the 1962 New York Titans did it against Denver. Williams had his midsection examined after the game, but said scans showed nothing wrong.
