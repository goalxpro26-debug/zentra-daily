A tanker slammed into a docked river cruise ship on the Mississippi River in New Orleans on Friday, tipping the smaller vessel on its side and partly submerging it as rescue crews pulled passengers from the water.

The Atlantic Asphalt tanker struck American Cruise Lines' American Heritage at about 12:30 p.m. local time while the ship was docked at the Port of New Orleans, port officials said. About 129 passengers were aboard, along with crew members. The ship had docked about two hours earlier to begin letting passengers off.

Video from CNN affiliate WDSU showed the tanker ramming the docked cruiser and pushing it over until it lay on its side, partly underwater, near the Crescent City Connection bridge. Twelve people were reported in the water in the first hours after the collision, and WDSU reported that a dozen people were taken to hospital. The Port of New Orleans said the number of injuries had not yet been confirmed.

Port NOLA Harbor Police, the New Orleans Fire Department, the New Orleans Police Department, the U.S. Coast Guard and emergency medical personnel all responded to the scene, according to a port news release. Passengers were escorted into the Port of New Orleans Administration Building, where they could be medically evaluated and given assistance.

## A rescue still unfolding

American Cruise Lines said its guests and crew had been evacuated and that it was in close coordination with the Coast Guard and local authorities. "We are deeply concerned for everyone involved," the company said in a statement.

Louisiana Governor Jeff Landry said he was aware of the incident and would be heading to New Orleans. New Orleans Mayor Helena Moreno's office said multiple people had been rescued and that search-and-rescue operations were continuing into the afternoon.

The cause of the collision is not yet known, and investigators were still working to establish how a tanker under way on the river came to strike a ship tied up at the dock. Officials have not said whether all passengers and crew have been accounted for, or how badly the American Heritage was damaged.

## What is still unknown

This remains a developing incident. The Port of New Orleans said its immediate priority was the safety of passengers, crew members and first responders, and that the situation was still evolving. The number of people injured, the condition of those taken to hospital, and the cause of the collision are all still to be confirmed by officials.
