More than 12,000 people were evacuated from a concert in Manchester on Sunday night after an unknown substance was released into the crowd, police said.

The incident happened towards the end of a performance by US rapper Don Toliver at the Co-op Live arena, at about 22:00. Greater Manchester Police declared a major incident after reports of concertgoers suffering the effects of the substance, and emergency services moved in to clear the venue.

One person was taken to Manchester Royal Infirmary for further assessment, according to the North West Ambulance Service. More than 50 people were assessed for eye and respiratory symptoms, and several were treated at the scene. Decontamination was carried out as a precaution, and the ambulance service deployed several emergency crews, including its Hazardous Area Response Team.

Assistant Chief Constable Steph Parker said the major incident declaration allowed emergency services to coordinate a safe response. Police said their priority was the safety of everyone at the concert, and thanked the crowd for its patience as the arena was emptied.

What exactly was released remains unknown. Police initially believed the substance to be tear gas, but that has not been confirmed, and officers said they would now work to establish what it was and how the incident came about.

The venue said it was working closely with police to identify the cause. In a statement, Co-op Live said the safety and security of visitors remained its priority and that it would share more information once it had an update from police.

Police advised anyone who had left the event and later became unwell to attend their local hospital, and the ambulance service gave the same advice to anyone who noticed side effects after leaving.

Toliver was performing in Manchester as part of his Nitrous tour. The show was stopped as the evacuation got under way, and footage shared on social media showed emergency services converging on the arena as concertgoers streamed out, some of them coughing.

The investigation is continuing, and no further details about the substance or how it was released have been given.
