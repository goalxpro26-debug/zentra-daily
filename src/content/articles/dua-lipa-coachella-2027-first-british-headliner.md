Dua Lipa will headline Coachella in 2027, becoming the first solo female British artist to top the bill at the California festival.

Coachella announced its 2027 line-up on Tuesday, with Lipa closing the Friday bill on both weekends, on 9 and 16 April. The Australian psych-rock band Tame Impala will headline the Saturday shows, and the American singer SZA will close out both Sundays. The festival returns to the Empire Polo Club in Indio, California, across two weekends — 9 to 11 April and 16 to 18 April — with more than 100,000 fans expected to attend each day.

## A first for a British woman

Lipa, whose hits include "Training Season", becomes the first British woman to headline Coachella. Charli XCX performed on the main stage in 2025, but ahead of that year's headliner, Green Day. Lipa's booking ends weeks of speculation about the 2027 headliners, after reports last month that she had been in advanced talks with the organisers.

The announcement also sets up a reunion with SZA: the two singers both headlined Glastonbury in 2024, and will now share top billing across the two Coachella weekends.

## Who else is playing

The line-up reaches well beyond the three headliners. Madonna is set to appear in the dance tent, alongside a bill that includes Daddy Yankee, Zara Larsson, Steve Lacy, Kehlani, JISOO of BLACKPINK, Peggy Gou and Giorgio Moroder. Phoebe Bridgers, Weezer, Tiësto, Martin Garrix, Role Model, Kygo, The Neighbourhood and Becky G are also on the roster, with Diljit Dosanjh among the international acts.

SZA's headline slot had been widely anticipated after she appeared to confirm it herself on social media in the run-up to the announcement, posting about the other two headliners in a public comment.

## What happens next

Detailed stage assignments and individual set times have not yet been released, and are expected to follow closer to the festival. For now, the headline news is the bill itself: three acts from three different corners of pop, with a British first at the top of it.
