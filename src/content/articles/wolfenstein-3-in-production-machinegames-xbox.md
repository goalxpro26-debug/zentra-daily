Wolfenstein 3 is officially in production at MachineGames, Xbox chief executive Asha Sharma announced on Wednesday, ending nearly a decade of waiting for the next entry in the alternate-history shooter series. The confirmation came as Xbox marked its 25th anniversary in Seattle, closing out an eight-city FanFest tour in the city where the brand began.

Sharma shared the news in a post on X, calling Wolfenstein "one of gaming's most important franchises" and praising the studio's work in carrying it forward. The post included a photo of a script page for the game's first chapter, titled "Nazitraz," according to Insider Gaming's account of the announcement. MachineGames followed minutes later with its own post: "His reputation precedes him. Wolfenstein 3 is in production."

Xbox's official recap of the anniversary announcements added a key detail: actors Brian Bloom and Debra Wilson are reprising their roles as BJ Blazkowicz and Grace Walker, the hero and the resistance leader of the modern series.

## Finishing the trilogy

MachineGames rebooted Wolfenstein in 2014 with The New Order, followed by The New Colossus in 2017. Studio director Jerk Gustafsson has said publicly that the team wanted to finish the story as a trilogy, while declining to say when. Wednesday's announcement is the first formal confirmation that the third game is actually being made.

It is also the first Wolfenstein title developed by MachineGames while the studio is under Xbox ownership, following Microsoft's acquisition of Bethesda parent company ZeniMax.

## What we still don't know

For all the excitement, the announcement was a confirmation, not a reveal. There is no trailer, no gameplay footage, no release date, no confirmed platforms and no subtitle beyond the chapter title on the script page. Xbox has said only that the game is in production and that it looks forward to sharing more.

A casting call reported earlier this year suggested performance capture was scheduled to run into 2027, pointing to a long wait — though none of that has been confirmed as part of the finished game.

## Part of a bigger anniversary day

Wolfenstein 3 was the headline gaming announcement in a day of Xbox news. Sharma also confirmed id Software's next project is in development, Halo's cooperative Firefight mode is coming to Halo: Campaign Evolved, and 25 original Xbox games are now available on PC. New Fable gameplay was shown as well.

For Wolfenstein fans, though, the day belonged to a single sentence: the trilogy is back on. After nine years of silence since The New Colossus, the third game is no longer a hope or a rumour. It is in production.
