Masked immigration officers detained two passengers on a jet bridge at Seattle-Tacoma International Airport on Saturday morning as they boarded an Alaska Airlines flight to Los Angeles, delaying the departure and prompting sharp criticism from the airline, the airport operator and Washington's governor.

The confrontation unfolded during boarding for Alaska Airlines Flight 1306, which had been scheduled to depart at 8:10 a.m. Video showed officers dragging away at least one person as a woman screamed that her daughter was being taken and that she has a work permit; officers were also seen struggling with a person on the ground.

The Department of Homeland Security said officers carried out a targeted enforcement operation against Ricardo Larrondo Mendoza, a Mexican national who, it said, had overstayed his visa. A second passenger, Sofia Hernandez Mejia, was also arrested after, according to the department, she threw hot coffee at officers during the confrontation. The department said she had also overstayed her visa and will face federal charges of assaulting a federal officer. No criminal charge was mentioned for Mendoza. A department source told CBS News the two are father and daughter.

Alaska Airlines said immigration officers gave it no advance notice of the enforcement action, which took place during a busy travel weekend ahead of the Thanksgiving holiday. "We are concerned by this morning's law enforcement action during the boarding of Alaska Flight 1306," the airline said, adding that it recognised how distressing the incident had been for affected employees and guests. The airline said it was working with the Port of Seattle, which manages the airport, to see whether protocols were followed, and that it had raised concerns directly with the appropriate authorities.

The Port of Seattle said the arrest was distressing not only for the affected family but for other passengers, and that local authorities would engage federal officials to understand more about what occurred.

Washington Governor Bob Ferguson, a Democrat, called the incident "just the latest example of ICE's deeply un-American tactics" and said the agency was "completely and totally out of control." He said he was outraged by masked agents who do not identify themselves dragging people off jetways as they board a flight.

Democratic Representative Adam Smith said masked agents detained at least one passenger and forcibly dragged the individual off the jet bridge. Representative Pramila Jayapal said she was disturbed by the reports and that her office was gathering more information.

The arrests come as immigration officers have stepped up enforcement at airports since the summer, focusing on visa overstays amid closer collaboration between ICE and the Transportation Security Administration — putting boarding gates and jet bridges at the centre of a growing political fight over where enforcement should happen.

It remains unclear how the two passengers were identified during boarding, or whether any airport protocols were breached. The episode is likely to add to scrutiny of the agency's tactics as Democrats prepare to take control of Congress and investigate them.
