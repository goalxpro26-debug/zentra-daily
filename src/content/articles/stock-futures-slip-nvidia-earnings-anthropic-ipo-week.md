Asian shares fell on Monday and US stock futures slipped, as investors braced for a week that could decide whether the artificial-intelligence rally still has further to run. Nvidia reports quarterly earnings on Tuesday, and Anthropic — the company behind the Claude chatbot — is due to start trading on Thursday.

The selling was broad across Asia. MSCI's broadest index of Asia-Pacific shares outside Japan dropped 1.9%, dragged down by a 4.7% tumble in Taiwan and a 3% fall in South Korea. Japan's Nikkei fell 1.7% after data showed the economy unexpectedly shrank last quarter. Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co., the main maker of Nvidia's AI chips, fell 4%, and China's blue-chip shares slid 0.6%. Hong Kong's Hang Seng index was the exception, rising 0.8%.

US futures pointed lower as well. S&P 500 futures slipped 0.2% and Nasdaq futures lost 0.4% on Monday, while European stock futures were up 0.2%.

## Nvidia's first test without Huang

Tuesday's results will be Nvidia's first earnings report since the shock departure of chief executive Jensen Huang last week. The chipmaker, the poster child of a four-year US bull market, has seen its market value fall to $3.9 trillion as of Friday, from about $5.8 trillion in early October, according to LSEG data, as investors rushed for the exits.

Analysts expect earnings growth of about 90% on revenue of $109 billion in Nvidia's fiscal third quarter. But the numbers may matter less than the message: all eyes are on whether the new management, led by interim chief executive Colette Kress, will stick to the chipmaker's bullish outlook for next year. Options markets imply a move of roughly 7% in either direction on the day.

"Nvidia may well deliver another exceptional beat and raise at tomorrow's earnings, but investors are increasingly trying to value a business in FY2028 and FY2029 potentially without the individual who architected its competitive advantage," said Chris Weston, head of research at brokerage Pepperstone.

## Anthropic's debut follows two days later

Trading in Anthropic is scheduled to begin on Thursday, just two days after Nvidia's results, setting up a real-time barometer for sentiment on the AI trade. The listing is expected to raise as much as $64.4 billion, at a fully diluted valuation of up to $1.4 trillion, according to a filing — and Reuters reported the IPO has drawn more institutional interest than SpaceX.

The week holds other tests for markets. Minutes from the US Federal Reserve's October policy meeting are due on Wednesday, and futures markets now treat a quarter-point interest rate hike next month as essentially locked in. In commodities, Brent crude fell 0.7% to $95.14 a barrel, while gold eased 0.2% to $3,872 an ounce.

For investors, the sequence is unforgiving: two verdicts on the AI trade, three days apart, with the Fed's thinking in between. By Friday, the market will know whether the rally's two biggest names still command the confidence they did a month ago.
