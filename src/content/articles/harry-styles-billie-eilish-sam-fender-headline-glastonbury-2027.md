Glastonbury Festival will return in 2027 with Harry Styles, Sam Fender and Billie Eilish headlining the Pyramid Stage, the organisers announced on Wednesday. Cher will play the coveted Sunday afternoon legends slot, becoming the oldest act ever to take it, at 81.

Sam Fender will open the headline run on Friday night, Styles will take the Pyramid Stage crown on Saturday, and Eilish will close the festival on Sunday. The three are the youngest group of Pyramid headliners since 1997 — Fender and Styles are both 32, Eilish is 24.

For Styles, it will be a Glastonbury debut. He arrives fresh from a 30-show residency at Madison Square Garden in New York. Fender, who last played the festival in 2022, takes a top spot for the first time, while Eilish returns to headline for a second time after her 2022 set made her the youngest solo artist ever to top the Pyramid bill.

Cher's legends slot is often treated as a fourth headline set. She will be 81 by the time of the festival, passing Rod Stewart, who played the slot at 80 in 2025. It will be her only UK festival appearance of the year.

The festival runs from Wednesday, June 23 to Sunday, June 27, 2027, at Worthy Farm in Somerset, with music on the main stages starting on Friday, June 25. It is the first Glastonbury in two years: the festival took 2026 off as a fallow year, the periodic break that lets the farm's pastures recover from hosting more than 200,000 people.

Tickets had already sold out before any headliner was named — in 42 minutes last month. Organiser Emily Eavis said the team was happy to be returning after the fallow year and wanted to offer fans an early taste of what is to come.

The first wave of names goes well beyond the headliners. Electronic pioneers Kraftwerk will headline the West Holts stage, and the bill so far also includes Massive Attack, Chase & Status, the xx, Lily Allen, The Streets, Kacey Musgraves, Primal Scream, Sombr, Sienna Spiro and Adéla. More names, stage-by-stage schedules and set times are expected in the New Year, along with a spring resale of any returned tickets.
