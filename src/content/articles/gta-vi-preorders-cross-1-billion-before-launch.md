Grand Theft Auto VI has already crossed an estimated $1 billion in pre-order revenue, a week before the game is due to launch on November 19 — a milestone its predecessor needed three days on sale to reach.

Analytics firm Sensor Tower puts pre-orders at about 10.31 million copies, according to reports published on November 11. The figures are estimates, not official sales numbers: neither Rockstar Games nor its parent company, Take-Two Interactive, has confirmed them.

## What the estimates say

PlayStation 5 accounts for roughly three-quarters of the pre-orders, at an estimated 7.73 million copies worth about $751 million, with Xbox Series X|S making up the remaining 2.58 million. Sensor Tower has tracked pre-orders on both consoles since they opened on June 25, and has not published the methodology behind its figures.

The pace has been quickening as launch approaches. On November 9 alone, the estimate grew by about 620,000 copies and roughly $59.5 million, according to the firm's tracker.

Take-Two reported its quarterly results on November 9 and gave no pre-order number, but chief executive Strauss Zelnick said demand was running "far in excess" of the company's expectations. The company expects net bookings of $8 billion to $8.2 billion for the fiscal year ending March 31, 2027, and $3.35 billion to $3.5 billion in the quarter in which the game launches.

## A record-breaking run-up

Sony has confirmed that Grand Theft Auto VI is now the most pre-ordered game in the history of the PlayStation Store, with Microsoft reporting a similar record on Xbox. Digital pre-loading opened on November 10, letting buyers download the game — a roughly 208GB install — ahead of release day.

The comparison with Grand Theft Auto V underlines the scale. Take-Two said in 2013 that GTA V generated more than $800 million in its first 24 hours and passed $1 billion within three days. But the two numbers are not measured the same way: the GTA V figure was the company's own estimate of sales in shops, while the GTA VI figure is an outside estimate of digital pre-orders on two consoles.

## What is still unknown

The first official figure will come from Take-Two after November 19, and it may not match Sensor Tower's estimate. The game arrives after two delays — it was first slated for fall 2025, then pushed to May 2026, and finally to November 19 — and expectations are high enough that analysts say a breakout launch would likely force the company to raise its earnings guidance.
