Russian ballistic missiles struck two of Kyiv's main bridges overnight, the city's mayor said, in an attack that damaged critical infrastructure and disrupted water, power and heating supplies in parts of the Ukrainian capital.

Kyiv Mayor Vitali Klitschko said the Northern and Southern bridges, which span the Dnipro River, had been hit. Loud explosions were heard across the city shortly before midnight, and Klitschko said soon afterwards that infrastructure systems had been damaged. Some of the city's above-ground subway services were also damaged, officials said, after residents were told to take shelter in civil defence shelters. Kyiv's underground stations are routinely used as shelters during air raids.

No casualties were reported in the capital from the bridge strikes themselves. Three people were injured in a separate Russian missile attack in the Cherkasy region, according to local officials.

## Why the bridges matter

Eight major bridges cross the Dnipro, linking western and eastern Kyiv, and an estimated three million residents rely on them. Several were also targeted by Russian drone strikes in October.

## A day after the largest drone attack

The strikes came a day after Russia said it had faced the largest Ukrainian drone attack since the war began. Russian officials said the military intercepted and destroyed 2,049 drones and four Flamingo long-distance missiles over 24 hours. Moscow's mayor was cited as saying around 600 drones flew toward the Russian capital alone.

The attack also followed a round of online talks between Ukrainian, US and European officials aimed at ending the war. President Volodymyr Zelensky had warned earlier that Moscow might launch further strikes on Monday night. In an address on Monday evening, he described the talks meeting as "difficult" and said Moscow was receiving "dozens of missiles per month" from North Korea.

US envoy Jared Kushner said after Monday's meeting that Washington was committed to providing Ukraine with "strong, credible security guarantees" and a path toward EU membership, with preparations under way for a further multilateral meeting. Zelensky said Europe was represented for the first time by the full delegation that will take part in negotiations with Moscow.

Elsewhere overnight, the Moscow-installed governor in Crimea said Ukraine had struck the Kerch Bridge linking Russia to occupied Crimea again, for a second consecutive day, and that traffic across the bridge had been suspended. The severity of the latest strike on that bridge was still unclear.

## What is still unknown

The BBC report did not say how badly the two Kyiv bridges were damaged, whether either crossing is closed, or how long repairs might take. It also did not report casualties in Kyiv from the overnight attack. Russia launched its full-scale invasion in February 2022; about a fifth of Ukraine is now under Russian occupation, and Kyiv has refused Moscow's demand to give up areas of the eastern Donbas that remain under Ukrainian control.
