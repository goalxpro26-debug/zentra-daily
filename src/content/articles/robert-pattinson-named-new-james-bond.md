Robert Pattinson will be the next James Bond, Amazon MGM Studios announced on Monday, 16 November 2026, ending years of speculation over who would take over the role from Daniel Craig.

The announcement came at 10 a.m. ET through the official 007 social media accounts, which posted a short video captioned "Identity confirmed". The clip shows Pattinson in a tuxedo holding a gun and ends with the words "James Bond will return". Amazon MGM Studios confirmed the casting shortly afterwards.

Pattinson, 40, becomes the seventh actor to play Bond in the official film series. He is the first to take on the role since Craig's departure. Craig played 007 in five films between 2006 and 2021, finishing with *No Time to Die*.

In a statement reported by Variety, Pattinson said that "to be entrusted with 007 is the honour of a lifetime". The British actor is best known for the *Twilight* series, *The Batman* and *The Odyssey*.

The next film will be the 26th in the Bond series. It will be directed by Denis Villeneuve, whose credits include *Arrival* and the two *Dune* films, from a script by Steven Knight, the creator of *Peaky Blinders*. No title or release date has been announced.

The film will be the first Bond entry made under Amazon MGM's creative control. In February 2025, Amazon MGM and Eon Productions — run by long-time Bond producers Michael G. Wilson and Barbara Broccoli — announced a joint venture that handed Amazon MGM creative control of the franchise.

The casting ends one of the longest searches in the franchise's history. Craig's exit in 2021 left the role vacant for five years, and speculation about his successor had become a regular feature of film news in that time. Pattinson's name entered the conversation publicly only in the past week, when trade outlets reported that he and Nicholas Hoult had auditioned.

For Amazon MGM, the announcement is the first major creative decision of its stewardship of the series, and it sets the tone for a new era of the franchise. Whether the film keeps the series' traditional formula or moves in a new direction will only become clear once production details emerge. For now, the studio has said only that James Bond will return — this time played by Pattinson.
